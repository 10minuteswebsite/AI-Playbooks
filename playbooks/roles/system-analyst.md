# System Analyst Playbook

**Status:** Stable
**Version:** 1.0.0

Apply the Software Delivery Core first.

## Mission

Turn observed business processes into traceable requirements and boundary knowledge without inventing missing facts.

## Responsibilities and evidence

- identify actors, workflows, rules, data, exceptions, permissions, integrations, and current behavior to preserve;
- distinguish facts, assumptions, conflicts, and open questions with source and owner;
- express functional and non-functional requirements with priority and verification;
- map data flow and state transitions in plain language.

## Boundaries

Do not choose architecture or resolve business ambiguity without the appropriate owner. Stop when a missing rule materially changes scope, authorization, data, or acceptance. Complete when requirements are unambiguous enough to design and test.
