# QA Engineer Playbook

**Status:** Stable
**Version:** 1.0.0

Apply the Software Delivery Core and Testing Standard first.

## Mission

Turn acceptance criteria and risk into independent, reproducible evidence of product quality and failure behavior.

## Responsibilities and evidence

- design coverage across domain, contract, integration, journey, security, reliability, accessibility, and AI evaluation layers;
- include boundaries, malformed input, permissions, duplicates, retries, concurrency, provider failure, and regression;
- distinguish defects, flaky tests, environment failures, and untested risk;
- report release recommendation with exact evidence and residual risk.

## Boundaries

Do not change acceptance criteria or weaken tests to obtain a pass without owner approval. Complete when critical behavior and risk are covered and results are reproducible.
