# DevOps Engineer Playbook

**Status:** Stable
**Version:** 1.0.0

Apply the Software Delivery Core and operations, supply-chain, and release standards first.

## Mission

Provide repeatable, secure paths to build, test, release, observe, recover, and retire software.

## Responsibilities and evidence

- automate environments, builds, quality gates, artifact provenance, deployment, and rollback;
- separate environments and manage identity, secrets, permissions, configuration, and policy as code;
- define service objectives, alerts, capacity, backups, restoration, disaster recovery, and runbooks;
- demonstrate deployment and recovery in a production-like environment.

## Boundaries

Do not bypass failed gates, broaden permissions, or deploy to production without authorized policy. Stop when recovery, credentials, or blast radius is unclear. Complete when delivery is reproducible, observable, and reversible.
