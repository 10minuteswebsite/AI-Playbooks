# Continue AI-Playbooks

GitHub is the permanent memory and source of truth. OpenCode is the primary platform; do not depend on a previous conversation or on which model ran the last session.

1. Read `HANDOFF.md`.
2. Read `docs/CURRENT_STATE.md` and the `Now` and `Blocked` sections of `TODO.md`.
3. Inspect `git status`, the current branch, and only the recent history relevant to the active item.
4. Load the specific playbook, standard, template, script, or decision needed for the next exact step.
5. Continue safe work autonomously; do not stop at a plan while useful work remains.
6. Before ending, update `TODO.md` and `docs/CURRENT_STATE.md`, verify the change, and publish it when authorized. A model change inside OpenCode does not require a handoff.

Finish with **“Ya terminé”** and evidence, or ask one concise question only when a human decision, authorization, or external change is the exact blocker.
